# CV Writing Rules

Use these rules when editing Yauheni's master profile, tailored CVs, HR messages, and LinkedIn text.

## Positioning

- Lead with Senior QA Automation Engineer / SDET.
- Treat DevOps as a QA advantage, not as a second target career.
- Frame infrastructure work through QA outcomes: reproducible environments, faster CI feedback, cleaner diagnostics, release confidence, and fewer manual support steps.
- Mirror the vacancy title in each tailored CV header when it matches the honest profile.
- Keep EU Blue Card, Krakow location, phone, email, and LinkedIn visible in recruiter-facing CVs.

## Claims

- Use only supported metrics.
- Strong supported metrics:
  - 7+ years in QA automation / SDET-style work.
  - Definely coverage increased from around 117 to 199 tests.
  - Definely automation logs reduced from around 1.5 GB / 1.69 GB to 11 MB.
  - Scenario validation improved from 5+ minutes to about 1 minute only when the source/job context supports that level of detail.
- Do not invent EPAM coverage numbers or adoption counts.
- Mark ramp-up areas honestly, especially contract testing, AWS depth, Cypress, and deep platform-level Kubernetes ownership.

## Style

- Cut generic AI phrasing.
- Prefer active verbs: refactored, built, automated, integrated, reduced, supported, investigated.
- Avoid filler such as relevant to, strong focus, practical debugging when it repeats across many CVs without detail.
- Keep bullets specific and short.
- Avoid dramatic contrasts, vague claims, and unsupported superlatives.
- Use categories for skills when humans will read the CV; keep categories compact for ATS parsing.

## Experience Shape

- Definely should show measurable automation impact early.
- Leapwork title should read as QA/SDET with DevOps and automation focus: QA Automation Engineer (DevOps & Automation Focus).
- Leapwork bullets should connect PowerShell, Azure DevOps, Bicep, Docker/Kubernetes, VM tooling, and monitoring to testing, release support, diagnostics, or environment reproducibility.
- EPAM should stay concise and foundation-focused unless a vacancy needs Java/C#/Selenium detail.
